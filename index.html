03 / What this result means

Low (0–29): Few of the selected warning signs were detected.

Medium (30–59): Some warning signs were detected. Check the link carefully.

High (60–100): Several warning signs were detected. Avoid interacting with the link unless independently verified.

This prototype uses local URL-pattern rules, not live threat-intelligence feeds or machine learning. A low score does not prove that a website is legitimate, and a high score does not prove malicious intent.

Link Shield is an educational demonstration. It analyzes the text structure of a URL only; it does not visit websites, verify ownership, or guarantee safety.
